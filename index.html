Engineering Portfolio

I study engineering and astrophysics at Swarthmore College. My work so far has included plasma hardware, particle-orbit simulations, and a few robotics projects. I’m especially interested in the hardware behind spaceflight: designing it, building it, and finding out how it behaves in testing.

In summer 2027, I’ll be joining SpaceX’s Starship Pad Engineering team at Cape Canaveral as an engineering intern. I’m excited to work on launch hardware at that scale, learn from people who know it inside and out, and hopefully come away a much better engineer.

About Me

Space has been my thing since I was a kid. I remember seeing the Moon through my first telescope, visiting Kennedy Space Center, and watching the James Webb Space Telescope launch years later. I still get excited about all of it. It's why I ended up studying engineering and astrophysics: I want to help build the things that make exploration possible.

I'm from the Jersey Shore. Outside of school, I spend a lot of time with friends and family, playing guitar, listening to music, and reading. I'm also an uncle, which is a pretty great gig. East of Eden is my favorite book, and I'm always up for a recommendation.
